01 — about

A little about me

I grew up in Goodland, and now I help keep its school district online. As IT Assistant for USD 352, I run the help desk and look after everything behind it — phones and intercoms, door access and cameras, and the 1,000+ devices staff and students use every day.

Alongside that I'm a full-time student at Fort Hays State University, working toward a B.S. in Information Networking and Telecommunications. Routing and switching coursework sits next to the databases and web projects I build on my own time, so I'm about as comfortable in a switch CLI as I am in a code editor.

The part I like most isn't the technical part. Explaining a fix so it actually sticks matters just as much as the fix did.
